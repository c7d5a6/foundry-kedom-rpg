# TEMP — Nerlander backgrounds (Náirland)

Scratch from site generator (`Backgrounds.vue` → `nerland`). Skill labels from `lang/ru.json`.

**Контакт** varies by life: **Дварфы** (craft/trade), **Полурослики** (settled neighbours), **Нитольцы** (roads & sea gossip), **Гномы** / **Гоблины** (wild fringe), **Хвирья** (cult & lore). Not every row is Дварфы or Торговля.

**Procedure (site):** `1d20` or choose → free skill → `1d8`×2 on growth.  
`any combat` = Удар / Стрельба / Холодное оружие · `any skill` = any of the 19.

**Growth rules:** same skill may appear twice only with *different* specialities; if the free skill is S, growth lists S at most once.
